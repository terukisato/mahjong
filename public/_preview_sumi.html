What works

On the felt the plates almost disappear until something happens to them — the watermark reads as texture, not a label, and the eye goes straight to the tiles. When the stroke lights up it's unmistakable against the green.

The turn / tenpai clash

Both are gold. Side by side (you, bottom · Ishida, left) the static tenpai stroke and the re-inking turn stroke are only told apart by motion — fine on a big screen, risky on a phone or with reduced motion. Turn may need a second cue: the name brightening, or a different treatment entirely.

Rotation & long titles

Nakamura, top: "Disciplined Safe Player" upside down still reads, clipped to one line. The 西 watermark rotates with it and stays legible. The side seats (90°) are the weakest — a vertical brush-stroke reads less clearly as a state than a horizontal one.
