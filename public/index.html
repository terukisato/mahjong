Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we store

Your display name; a salted hash of your password (never the plain text); your chosen icon, title, nameplate skin, and voice theme; your coin balance and the cosmetics you've bought; your game history, stats, and achievements; and your social data: your friends list, friend requests, and the players you've recently played with. No email address or other personal information is collected.

Why

Solely to run the game: logging you in, showing your stats and history, and powering friends, invites, and the store. Your display name, icon, title, and nameplate are shown to other players in games, friends lists, and leaderboards, along with your leaderboard stats.

Cookies & local storage

The game sets no cookies. It stores a "stay logged in" token and your settings (sound, music, rules, and appearance) in your browser's local storage, and briefly keeps in-progress game info in session storage so you can rejoin after a page reload. These are needed for the game to work and are never used for tracking. No analytics, ads, or third-party trackers.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads (see Google's privacy policy). The game is hosted on Railway, which logs IP addresses and request details for security and operations; these logs are kept for a limited time.

Your choices

You can ask to have your account and its data deleted at any time by contacting the developer.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the game evolves; the "Last updated" date above will change whenever it does.
